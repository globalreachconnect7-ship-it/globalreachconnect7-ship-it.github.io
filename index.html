ClearPath Account Guide

Guidance for finding old retirement accounts and unclaimed money.

Welcome

Have you changed jobs or moved and lost track of an old retirement account? You may be able to use official US government resources to search for information.

ClearPath Account Guide helps people understand where to begin their search.

1. Search for an old 401(k)

The US Department of Labor offers a Retirement Savings Lost and Found tool to help people search for information about retirement plans connected to previous jobs.

Visit Official Retirement Search

Access information: The official retirement search may not be available in every country. If you cannot access it, visit the US Department of Labor website for more information.

2. Search for unclaimed money

You may have unclaimed funds held by a state government or another institution. Use the official US government guide to find the appropriate search service.

Find Official Search Resources

3. Need more information?

Visit the US Department of Labor's Employee Benefits Security Administration for information about workplace retirement benefits.

Visit US Department of Labor

Important Information

This website is an independent informational guide. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the US government.

We cannot guarantee that you have money waiting for you. Official searches may be free.

Never share your Social Security number, password, bank details, or verification codes with this website.
