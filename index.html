How It Works

Three simple steps to help you begin.

  1. Choose a category. Identify whether you are looking for retirement benefits, unclaimed property, a pension, or life insurance.
  2. Visit the official resource. Follow the relevant website's instructions and eligibility requirements.
  3. Confirm any possible match. Contact the relevant agency, insurer, state office, or plan administrator directly.
Protect your personal information: Never send us your Social Security number, bank details, passwords, or verification codes. Enter sensitive information only on a verified official website when required. A search result does not guarantee that money is owed.
